# Possum Run: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

Possum Run ("the app") is a free game by Wise Owl Tech ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your data.

## What we collect
**We do not collect, store or receive any personal data.** There are no accounts, no analytics and no servers operated by us. Your high score, lifetime grubs and progress are saved only on your device and never leave it. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
The app shows ads using Google AdMob. Google and its ad partners may collect and use data from your device, such as your **advertising ID**, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions, to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and (if you consent) personalise ads. See [How Google uses data](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

- A short ad may appear between levels, and you can optionally watch a rewarded video for an in-game bonus.
- **Consent:** where required (for example the EEA, UK and some US states) the app shows Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form before any ads load. You can change your choice later where the app offers a privacy-options entry or in Android Settings.
- **Controls:** reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in Android *Settings > Google > Ads*.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Data sharing and security
We do not sell or share data, because we hold none. Ad traffic uses encrypted connections.

## Changes
We may update this policy; the date above will change.
